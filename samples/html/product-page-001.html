ProtoBoard X1 — Open-Source Dev Board

$49.00

ProtoBoard X1 is a compact, open-source development board built around a 32-bit microcontroller. USB-C power and data, integrated Wi-Fi 6 radio, and a 40-pin header for prototyping. Schematics, Gerbers, and bootloader source are released under permissive licenses.

Connectivity
USB-C (data + power), 40-pin GPIO header
Power
5 V via USB-C, 3.3 V regulated rail, deep-sleep < 50 uA
Dimensions
54 x 28 x 6 mm
